04 / SELECTED PROJECTS

01SUMMER REFRESHER · TEAM PROJECT

Mortality pooling and longevity risk in life annuity pricing

Worked in a four-person team to study how portfolio size and changes in mortality affect the cost of an immediate life annuity. We compared expected present values with simulated liabilities, then tested lower mortality rates across the portfolio.

10,000 simulationsMonte Carlo mean within 0.083% of the analytical expected present value
  • Modelled annual payments of $6,500 for a male entering at age 60, using a life table and a fixed 4% interest rate.
  • Examined how pooling reduces individual mortality risk, while a shared change in mortality still affects expected liabilities.
  • Under a 9% reduction in mortality rates at every age, expected liability rose from about $91,576 to $93,523 per policy.

These are illustrative model results. The analysis assumes independent, homogeneous policyholders and excludes expenses, profit margins, taxes, and capital requirements.

  • Actuarial pricing
  • Monte Carlo simulation
  • Longevity risk
  • Stress testing

03MAR to APR 2026 · STAT 371

Explaining automobile prices with regression

Worked in a three-person team to examine which vehicle characteristics were associated with price using the UCI Automobile dataset and regression analysis in R.

0.907adjusted R² for the final log-price model
  • Prepared 199 observations from the original 205 vehicles, assessed missing values, and explored relationships among price, vehicle dimensions, engine specifications, and categorical features.

Modelling Craps with an absorbing Markov chain

Worked in a four-person team to model the pass-line bet in Craps as a six-state discrete-time Markov chain, assuming two fair, independent dice.

49.29%theoretical probability of winning the modelled bet
  • Defined the start, point, win, and loss states and constructed a transition probability matrix.
  • Used matrix powers to study short-run outcomes and analysed transient and absorbing states to calculate eventual win probabilities.

The model covers one type of bet; other Craps bets have different probabilities and payoffs.
