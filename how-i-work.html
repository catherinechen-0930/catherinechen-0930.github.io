How I work

The skills I bring to my work, with examples of how I’ve put them into practice.

Practical problem-solving

MPAC · TDSB

At MPAC, I helped develop supplier risk evaluation tools. At TDSB, I built automated vendor tracking and reporting solutions. In both roles, I looked for where a process was slowing people down, used data to understand the problem, and worked on practical improvements.

Clear communication

UWaterloo · TDSB · MPAC

As a web assistant, I asked for clarification when I needed it and contributed to team discussions. At TDSB, I handled shared-mailbox inquiries and communicated directly with vendors. At MPAC, I worked across business units on workflow improvements and training materials, adapting the information to the people using it.

Taking ownership

TDSB

While organizing a shared mailbox, I noticed that a long-term vendor’s concerns had gone unanswered. I drafted a professional apology, brought the issue to my manager, and helped coordinate a prompt response. The experience reinforced how much trust depends on acknowledging a concern and following through.

Supporting the team

UWaterloo

After completing my assigned web pages ahead of schedule, I volunteered to help teammates with their remaining work. This helped move the website migration forward. It taught me to look beyond my own task list and pay attention to what the team still needs.

Staying organized

UWaterloo · TDSB

At UWaterloo, I balanced website development, quality assurance, and project support. I later drew on that experience at TDSB, where I managed more than 50 emails a day alongside website updates and onboarding and approval support for over 200 vendors. Keeping track of competing priorities helped me stay on top of deadlines.

Learning & adapting

Co-op roles · Personal projects

Moving between web development, enterprise risk, and procurement meant learning new tools and business processes in each role. Outside work, I built a Bilibili channel from scratch and taught myself video editing, content planning, and audience engagement. I refined my approach using audience feedback, and one video received over 9,000 likes.

Quantitative thinking

Coursework · Research

My studies in statistics, financial analysis, and risk management gave me a foundation for quantitative work. In a research project, I transformed qualitative evaluations into structured numerical data and built a multi-layer perceptron (MLP) model in R to predict investment recommendations for small business projects. The model achieved 81.8% validation accuracy.

A focus on risk

UWaterloo · TDSB · MPAC · U of T

Studying Financial Analysis and Risk Management at UWaterloo introduced me to quantitative approaches to uncertainty. My co-op roles at TDSB and MPAC showed me how risk management informs governance, supplier management, and day-to-day decisions. I’m continuing to build on those experiences through the Master of Financial Insurance program at the University of Toronto.
